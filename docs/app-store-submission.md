## 4. Description

### English
Adro brings your food, training and sleep together, with an AI coach that knows your plan and your numbers.

FOOD
- Calories and macros worked out for your goal
- Snap a meal: the AI finds the ingredients and the grams
- Barcode scan and food search, including Saudi and Gulf foods
- Recipes from TikTok and Instagram videos
- Water and caffeine, with gentle reminders

TRAINING
- A weekly plan built for your days and your equipment
- Log sets with your last weights in grey, and coach tips after each set
- Warm-ups, records and workout history
- Cardio and sports, live with the AI
- Gym Bros: train together live with friends, with turns, cheers and a friendly push

AI COACH
- Ask anything about your food, training and sleep
- A weekly recap that checks your plan and adjusts it

YOUR BODY
- Weight, measurements, sleep, steps and heart rate
- Connects with Apple Health and your watch
- Cycle tracking
- Ramadan mode with iftar and suhoor times

REAL COACHES
- Find a personal or online coach, see their packages and ask them to coach you

Adro is free to use. Adro+ unlocks the full AI coach: unlimited chat, meal photos, label and blood test photos, video recipes and the weekly AI recap.

Adro+ renews until you cancel in your Apple ID settings, at least 24 hours before it renews.
Terms: https://adroapp.com/site/terms.html
Privacy: https://adroapp.com/site/privacy.html

## 6. Age rating (later)
Answer honestly; the parts that matter for Adro:
- User-generated content: **Yes** (Gym Bros, groups, coach chat). Users can report and block, and the team can hide content and suspend accounts.
- Messaging between users: **Yes**.
- Medical or treatment information: **No** (general fitness and food, not medical advice).
- Everything else (violence, gambling, etc.): **None**.

## 7. App Privacy ("Data used to track you": none)
Collected and **linked to the user**, not used for tracking:
- Contact info: name, email address, phone number (optional): app functionality
- Health & fitness: health (Apple Health readings, cycle), fitness (workouts, steps): app functionality
- User content: photos (meal, label, blood test photos), other user content (food logs, messages, notes): app functionality
- Identifiers: user ID: app functionality
- Purchases: purchase history (Adro+): app functionality
Not collected: location, browsing history, advertising data. No ads, no third-party tracking.
