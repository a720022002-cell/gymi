# Where to put the word adro with the centered ring

1. Under the ring, small (art_w_under.svg): the ring with a small off-white "adro" under it, 6 x 7 cm.
2. Back neck (art_w_word.svg): "adro" (white letters, blue ring) just under the hood, about 6 cm.
3. Hem tab (art_w_tab.svg): a small woven black tab with "adro" sewn into the front hem, 3.4 x 1.4 cm.
They can be combined (for example 2 + 3).
