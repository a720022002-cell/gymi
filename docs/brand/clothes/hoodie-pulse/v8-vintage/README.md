# Hoodie backs: vintage print style (owner's references)

Made with Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.6) on the owner's charcoal hoodie photo, in the style of a worn, distressed screen print: bold condensed text tilted up, a grainy halftone photo in cream, small adro logo top right. No people.

1. BEYOND WHAT YOU SEE (blue) over the Edge of the World cliffs at sunrise.
2. SHOW UP EVEN WHEN NOBODY IS WATCHING (cream) over a dumbbell under one light in an empty gym.
3. EVERY NIGHT COUNTS (blue, arched) over the Riyadh skyline at night.
4. CLOSER TO WHAT YOU FEEL (cream) over dunes, the moon replaced by the blue adro ring.

These are concepts. The chosen one is rebuilt as print files: halftone artwork at full size (300 dpi) plus the text and logo as vector.
