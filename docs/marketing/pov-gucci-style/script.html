TRANSITION GRAMMAR (from Gucci × Oura)

  • Match on action: a move starts in one shot and finishes in the next.
  • Door cuts: every door he pushes opens a new place (bathroom, gym).
  • Whip pans bridge places (scale → car, highway → drive-thru).
  • Speed ramps on the gym door and the second lift.
  • Graphic match of circles from top-down shots.
  • One VHS rewind at the donut box: the day rewinds and he chooses again.
  • Micro-cut bursts (0.2 s) for the office rush.
  • Echo shots: sink morning/night, nightstand morning/night.

SOUND

  • A warm, groovy beat that speeds up from morning to the gym and calms down at night. Every cut lands on the beat.
  • Foley in every shot: alarm, tap, water, engine, ice, pen, donut box, chalk, the bar shaking and dropping back, plates, family laughter.
  • A tape-rewind sound on the VHS moment. On the missed rep the music drops out and only his breathing and the shaking bar are heard, then it comes back on the second try.
  • Total silence when the lamp goes out, then a soft chime on the ring.

PRODUCTION & COST

  • ~30 generations (the rewind and the ending are built in edit). 3–4 s each; only the best ~0.5–1.3 s is used.
  • Start with one reference image (hand + watch + thobe cuff), used in every shot.
